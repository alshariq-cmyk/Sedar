Drop quotation reports here

The quotationsReport.xls files you download from Exiros, one per RFQ. Select or drop many at once. The RFQ number comes from the file name.

Quoted and not quoted

Lines with a unit price count as quoted. An RFQ with no prices shows as Not quoted; some prices, Partially quoted.

Deadlines

The report file has no closing date. Open an RFQ and set its deadline once; the board then sorts and colours it.

Updating an RFQ

Adding a newer report for the same RFQ replaces its lines and keeps the deadline you set.
