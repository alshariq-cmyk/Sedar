RFQ PLATFORM - HOW TO USE

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Install Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/ (tick "Add python.exe to PATH").
2. Unzip this folder, e.g. into Documents.
3. Double-click setup_windows.bat and wait for "Setup finished".

EVERY DAY
1. Double-click start_platform.bat. The RFQ Platform opens in your browser.
   Keep the black window open while you use it; close it to stop.
2. To get the latest RFQs, click "Update from Exiros".
   A browser window opens on the Exiros login page. Log in.
   The platform then collects every RFQ (quoted and not quoted) with deadlines,
   logs out of Exiros and closes that window by itself.
3. The platform never goes into Exiros unless you click that button and log in.

WHAT YOU SEE
- RFQs sorted by deadline: red = closes within 48 hours, orange = within 7 days.
- New RFQs are marked "New" until you open them.
- Click an RFQ for every line: item, quantity, your price, delivery, brand, notes.
- Price history: search an item to see what you quoted for it before.
- "Download all as Excel" for everything in one file. All amounts are in SAR.

PRIVACY
Everything stays on this computer (the data folder). Your Exiros password is only
typed into the Exiros page itself; the platform never sees or stores it.
